Your rep is counted only when the app sees the movement pattern. A camera estimate is not a perfect form check.

How to get better tracking
• Keep the phone steady, about 2–3 metres away, with your full body visible.
• Side view is usually best for push-ups, curls and squats; front view helps jumping jacks.
• Wear clothes that contrast with the background and use bright, even light.
• Pull-ups need a safe, stable bar and the whole upper body visible. This demo may not count every rep accurately.
• Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or unusual discomfort. Never force a rep to satisfy the counter.
